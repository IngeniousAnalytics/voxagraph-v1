Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Professional license key work? +

After purchase, you'll receive a license key that activates the downloadable version of Voxagraph. The key is tied to the number of database connections you've purchased (up to 5 for Professional). You can manage your licenses through your account dashboard.

Can I upgrade from Professional to Enterprise? +

Yes, you can upgrade at any time. We'll prorate the difference between your Professional plan and the Enterprise subscription. Contact our sales team for large deployments or special requirements.

What databases do you support? +

Voxagraph supports all major SQL databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle) and cloud data warehouses (BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift). We also support CSV/Excel file uploads for quick analysis.

How secure is my data in the demo? +

Demo uploads are processed in a secure, isolated environment and automatically deleted after 24 hours. For production data, we recommend the downloadable Professional or Enterprise versions which keep all data on your infrastructure.

What is Human-in-the-Loop and why is it important? +

Human-in-the-Loop is our unique feature that adds a human review step for critical queries. This ensures AI-generated SQL queries are validated before execution, preventing costly errors and maintaining data integrity. It's especially valuable for compliance and audit requirements.
